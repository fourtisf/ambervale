# Logo directions

Six ideas for the profile mark, all wearing the same coin treatment — bevelled
rim, recessed enamel field, one dome highlight — so they are judged on the idea
rather than on which one happened to get the nicer bevel.

`contact-sheet.png` shows all six at 400px with 48/32/16px tests beside each.
**The small sizes are the real test**, because that is the size an avatar is
actually seen at in a timeline, and it is where four of these six lose most of
what makes them interesting at full size.

| Mark       | Idea                                                 | Holds at 16px |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| The Drop   | Amber with a sprout in it. Geometric, high contrast. | Yes, best     |
| The Seal   | Sun over furrows, ringed with seed. Fully abstract.  | Yes           |
| The Farmer | The existing mark, cut to hat and face, no outline.  | Yes           |
| The Crest  | Heraldic shield, sun above one ear of wheat.         | Softens       |
| The Vale   | The landscape in the coin — sun, ridge, windmill.    | Mushy         |
| The Sheaf  | Three bound ears reading as an A.                    | Smudges       |

Two of these were rebuilt after the first render rather than shipped: the sheaf
came out reading as a **crown**, because the grains were round blobs on short
stalks; and the crest's charge was a windmill that collapsed into an **X** with
wheat under it that read as a bowl. Both faults were invisible in the source
and obvious the moment they were drawn at 400px.
